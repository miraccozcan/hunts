HUNTS

Real-life hide and seek, on a live map.

Draw the play area, invite your friends and go. Hunters chase, runners hide, and the zone keeps closing in.

  • Hunters and runners. Hunters are picked at random. Runners get a head start.
  • Pings. Every few minutes, the runners show up on the hunters' map.
  • A shrinking zone. Fewer places to hide as the match goes on.
  • Catches you can trust. The runner confirms on their own phone.
  • Team radio and voice alerts. Play with your phone in your pocket.
