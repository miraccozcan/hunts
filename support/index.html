Common questions

How do I play?

One person taps Host, draws the play area on the map and sets the rules. Friends join with the code, the QR code or Nearby. Hunters are picked at random and runners get a head start. Pings show runners on the hunters' map every few minutes, and the zone shrinks over time. A catch counts when the runner confirms it on their phone. To try it alone first, start a practice match against bots from the Play tab.

Why does HUNTS ask for my location “Always”?

So the match keeps working while your phone is locked in your pocket. HUNTS uses your location only while a match is running. You can choose “While Using the App” instead and keep the app open during matches. Turn on Precise Location: catches and the zone need your exact position.

The app says “Waking up the server…”

When nobody has played for a while, the game server can take up to a minute to start. Wait a moment and it continues on its own. Practice matches don't need the server.

How do I turn the voice alerts off?

In a match, open the menu (…) and switch off Voice alerts. You can also use Profile › Voice alerts.

How do I delete my HUNTS ID?

Go to Profile › Delete account. This deletes your account and everything the game server kept for it. Your settings and saved maps on the phone are removed when you delete the app.

Playing safely

Stop at every road and never run across traffic. Stay off highways, rail lines and private property. Play with people you know, in places you're allowed to be. Hosts can mark no-go zones on the map. Read Play safe in the app before your first match.
